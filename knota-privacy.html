Knota Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Our commitment: Knota does not collect, store, or transmit any user data. All your data is saved only on your device.

1. Data Collection

Knota does not collect any personal information, including:

  • Name, email, phone number or other identifiers
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data

2. Data Storage

All content you create in Knota (projects, yarn, notes, photos, etc.) is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad only. Nothing is uploaded to any server or cloud service.

3. Third-Party Services

Knota does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The app does not require an internet connection.

4. Data Export & Backup

The app provides a manual export feature that saves your data as a JSON file to a location you choose. This process is entirely under your control; Knota is not involved in transmitting or storing your data.

5. Children's Privacy

Knota is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, users will be notified through an app update and the date above will be updated.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please contact us via the developer page on the App Store.

© 2026 Knota. This privacy policy applies to the iOS version of Knota.
